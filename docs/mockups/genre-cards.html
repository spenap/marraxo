Three ways to show the genre families from the Nexus 4's library, drawn as still screens. Names and counts are real; covers are tinted stand-ins. A is the one I'd build first.

A · Home

A shelf of genre cards on Home

Each family or genre is a card you can play: shuffled, later as a radio. The line under it is the card's "why", which every card must have.

Recommended: it is the cards milestone, and families already made the shelf.
