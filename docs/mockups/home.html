Home: five questions, as mockups

Each section is one open question with its options side by side; pick one per section, or none. Albums, people and studios are from the collection and its liner notes; play counts and dates are made up. Each phone scrolls; shelves scroll sideways. “Lean” marks Claude’s suggestion, not a decision.
