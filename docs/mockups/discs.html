Recommendation: A, a row for each disc

It shows where each disc starts and how long it is, costs one extra row per disc in the list the app already draws, and stays out of the way on the 425 albums with a single disc (no row at all). Disc titles fit on the same row when there are any.

B adds pinning to A and can follow later if long boxes (four discs, 78 songs) feel lost. C hides half the album to show the other half. D is the cheapest and says the least.
