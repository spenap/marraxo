Genre tiles that change on each visit

Each tile shows one cover from its genre and takes its colour from that cover. A new cover is picked when you open the Genres tab from another tab; nothing moves while you look. Genre names and counts are from the Nexus 4; covers are stand-ins named after real albums in the library.

What it costs

One small cover per tile, the same size the lists already load, and recent covers stay in memory. Picking a different one is choosing an index. Nothing runs while the screen is still.

When covers change

Each time you open Genres from another tab. Going into a genre and back, or into an album and back, keeps them, so the screen doesn't shift under you while you browse.

Which covers

Only albums with artwork take part. A genre with none, like Soundtrack here, shows the note on the placeholder colour, as album rows do today.
