Nexus 4 music player · interactive prototype

Glow, screen by screen

A clickable version of the chosen direction, on a drawn Nexus 4 at its real proportions. Tap through the screens, use the Android back and home keys, press the power button on the right edge to lock the phone, and use the volume keys on the left.

Nothing plays sound and nothing touches the network. "Mako" is a working name. The albums are example data; durations are approximate.

Flows

First launch

Happens once. Later launches open straight into the Library, or Now Playing if something was playing.

Play an album

  3. tap a track

Tapping a track plays the album from that track. The mini player then stays at the bottom of every browsing screen.

Play on a speaker or the TV

  2. tap “Playing on…”
  4. choose Estudio

The phone serves the file over Wi-Fi and the speaker fetches it. The phone stays the remote, and its volume keys control the speaker.

Screen off

  1. power button
  3. controls work locked
  4. unlock

Playback runs in a background service, so locking, Home and Back never stop the music.

Drawn to the real device

  • Body 133.9 × 68.7 mm; screen 4.7″, 768 × 1280 px at xhdpi, so 1 CSS px here is 1 dp on the phone.
  • 24 dp status bar and 48 dp on-screen nav bar: the app gets 384 × 568 dp.
  • Power on the right edge, volume rocker on the left, notification LED under the screen. The LED pulses while the phone is locked and playing.
