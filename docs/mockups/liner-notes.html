Where the data comes from

MusicBrainz credits and Wikipedia summaries, fetched once on the laptop and copied to the phone with the music. 381 of 384 albums are matched, 234 have a story, 30,162 credits name 3,798 people and places. Nothing is fetched on the phone.

What's real and what's not

All names, roles, years and links are real. "London" next to The Town House is the one detail added by hand; the database holds the studio's name only. Covers are stand-ins.

Languages
